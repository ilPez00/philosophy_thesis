\begin{abstract}
AYU is a three-layer computational architecture designed for the recovery of the Subject as a causal root. In an epoch defined by the Mirror Trap—a structural entrapment where language and societal discourse mediate action into passive reflection—the fragmented Ego loses its capacity for agency. AYU restores sovereignty through an integrated infrastructure: Aura, a privacy-first observation layer; Praxis, a social-technical forge; and Rachmaninov, a translation protocol that aligns ontologies. This thesis maps a recursive, four-fold cosmology—One, Descent, Struggle, Ascent—across the Cosmic, Biological, Anthropic, and Individual grades of reality, presenting the AYU technical stack as a mimesis of cosmic metabolism.
\end{abstract}
