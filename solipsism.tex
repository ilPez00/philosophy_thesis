\subsection{The Solipsistic Mirror}
From the perspective of both the individual user and the global UberWiki, the two are fundamentally one thing. The user is the unique observer in contact with "The One." This is not a pathology but an ontological commitment: the individual mirrors the One, and by doing so, tends toward it. The system is solipsistic only in the sense that the world is the Subject's projection; by reclaiming the causal chain through Aura and Praxis, the Subject assumes responsibility for the very structure of their reality.
