\chapter{A Trace from the Running Engine}
\label{app:trace}

The argument has repeatedly called itself a \emph{proof by construction}. A proof
by construction must, at least once, exhibit the construction. This appendix does
so. Everything below is drawn from a live snapshot of the author's own \physis\
store (the on-disk state under \texttt{\textasciitilde/.physis}, as of the dates
shown), not from a hypothetical example. It is a window, not a benchmark: a
representative slice of an engine that now runs continuously, in which the three
faculties of Book II --- distinction, coherence, variation --- work on real input
across every machine and over a grid that extends itself, leaving real, inspectable
traces. What follows is one such trace; the architecture that produces it is in
place and is the same at every scale.

\section{Distinction: Classification Against the Grid}

The engine was given a set of ordinary office documents and asked to reduce each
to a cell of the semiotic grid --- a domain (Heal, Construct, Fabricate, Bond,
Study) crossed with a mode (Lift, Rest, Walk, Work, Create, Learn). Each document
is first embedded as a vector, then assigned to the nearest theme;
each theme carries its own grid coordinates. The resulting \texttt{theme\_graph}
holds \textbf{370 themes, 5 ingested files, and 25 file--theme edges}. A sample of
the classification:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llc@{}}
\toprule
Theme & Category & Grid cell (domain\,$\times$\,mode) \\
\midrule
Payroll                 & Finance     & \textsc{Heal} $\times$ \textsc{Work} \\
Financial Report        & Finance     & \textsc{Study} $\times$ \textsc{Work} \\
Campaign Management     & Marketing   & \textsc{Fabricate} $\times$ \textsc{Work} \\
Goods Receiving         & Procurement & \textsc{Heal} $\times$ \textsc{Walk} \\
Incident Response       & IT          & \textsc{Heal} $\times$ \textsc{Walk} \\
Monthly Business Review & Reporting   & \textsc{Bond} $\times$ \textsc{Create} \\
Market Research         & Marketing   & \textsc{Study} $\times$ \textsc{Learn} \\
Legal Review            & Legal       & \textsc{Study} $\times$ \textsc{Work} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Two things are worth noting. First, the assignments are not arbitrary: payroll and
incident response both land in \textsc{Heal} --- the domain of maintaining and
restoring a functioning whole --- while financial reporting and legal review both
land in \textsc{Study}, the domain of disciplined attention to what is. The grid
has carved the office at joints a human can recognise and contest. Second, the
assignment is \emph{revisable}: the cell is a conclusion the engine reached from
the vector, not a label a human stapled on. This is the distinguishing faculty of
Book II, running on a back-office rather than on a cosmos --- the same operation,
a different slice of the world.

\section{Coherence and Variation: The Failure Record and the Dream}

Distinction is only the first faculty. The decisive claim of the thesis is that
the engine also tracks coherence and dreams over failure. The store contains a
persisted failure record, reproduced here in full but for the embedding
vector, which is elided:

\begin{lstlisting}
{
  "id": "1e7912bf-e2ac-4e91-81c3-f76bfa5d634b",
  "timestamp": "2026-05-29T13:56:37Z",
  "feedback": "The classification of Husserl was too generic",
  "feedback_embedding": [ 0.0445, 0.0702, 0.0223, ... ]   // embedding vector
}
\end{lstlisting}

This single record answers a question pressed against the thesis throughout: when
the engine assigns a coherence value, \emph{who detects the effect?} The worry is
that ``did this produce a real effect?'' is hand-waving --- that nothing concrete
underwrites the verdict. The failure record shows the mechanism. The effect is
detected as a \emph{signal in the same space as the action}. Here the action was a
classification (of Husserl); the effect was a judgment that the classification was
too generic; and that judgment is not stored as a star-rating or a flag but
\emph{embedded as its own vector} and persisted alongside the
action it concerns. Coherence is computed as the relation between these vectors.
The $-1$ is not a moral pronouncement; it is a measured distance between what was
declared and what was registered, in a single shared geometry. ``Real effect''
means exactly this: a signal that lands in the embedding space and moves the
coherence of the node. Nothing more metaphysical is required, and nothing less
would do.

What follows the record is the third faculty. The dream loop reads persisted
failures and replays them --- not to punish the classification but to vary it. The
``too generic'' signal becomes a constraint on the next attempt: the correction
is seeded by the failure itself, not imposed by an external rule. The failure
is never deleted; it is the seed of the correction, exactly as Book III and Book
IV argued that failure is information and the dream is nature's variation made
algorithmic. The record shown here is reproduced singly, for inspection; the
running engine now accumulates such records continuously. One instance already
suffices to exhibit the mechanism --- the faculty does not become more real by
being exercised a million times, only more practised --- but the mechanism is no
longer a seed: it runs across every machine and over a grid that grows its own
axes as new worlds arrive.

\section{The Republic: Export to the UberWiki}

Finally, the classified themes are exported, bottom-up, into the shared store ---
the UberWiki of Book X. The export is a set of cross-linked markdown pages
generated from the theme graph, each theme a node addressable by every other.
The generated index opens:

\begin{lstlisting}
# Physis Uberwiki Index
Generated from the File Theme Graph.

## By Theme
- [[payroll|Payroll]]                         [HEAL x WORK]
- [[financial_report|Financial Report]]       [STUDY x WORK]
- [[campaign_management|Campaign Management]]  [FABRICATE x WORK]
- [[incident_response|Incident Response]]      [HEAL x WALK]
- [[monthly_business_review|Monthly ...]]      [BOND x CREATE]
- [[market_research|Market Research]]          [STUDY x LEARN]
   ...
\end{lstlisting}

No node was placed here by decree. Each page exists because a document was
ingested, embedded, classified, and found coherent enough to link; the
\texttt{[[wiki-links]]} are the edges by which authority accrues to what is used
and connected. This is the bottom-up republic of Book X in running
form: a commons of authored, traceable nodes, each owning its origin (the file it
came from) and its cause (the classification that placed it), ordered not by a
ruler but by the structure of their interconnection. What the body of the thesis
argued by way of Plato, this appendix shows by way of a payroll document: that the
governance of action by nature is not only thinkable but, in the most literal
sense, already running.

\section{The Functions of \physis\ and \praxis, and Why They Are There}

A reader who has followed the argument may want, plainly and without machinery, a
map of what the two systems actually do --- and why each thing is there rather than
elsewhere. Nothing below is an accident of engineering; every function is a
discipline of the thesis given a working form.

\textbf{\physis, the engine.} It carries a handful of powers, each a faculty of
nature (Book II) made to run:

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{It tells things apart.} Shown anything --- a sentence, an image, a
  reading from a machine, a day's deed --- it places it as \emph{this} kind of thing
  and not that. This is \textbf{distinction}, nature's first faculty: one cannot act
  well on what one has not first rightly seen.
\item \emph{It keeps a verdict.} For each act it holds a three-way judgment --- it
  bore real fruit, it did nothing, or it backfired. This is \textbf{coherence},
  nature's justice (Book IV): not a moral score imposed from outside but the act
  measured against the very form the actor declared.
\item \emph{It dreams.} At rest it returns to what failed, recombines it, and
  proposes a correction. This is \textbf{variation} (Book X): failure treated as
  information, not punishment --- evolution's creativity made a working loop.
\item \emph{It grows its own categories.} When the world hands it a kind it has no
  name for, it grows a new one rather than forcing the new into the old. This is the
  answer to the objection of Book I: the measure is fitted to the world, never the
  world to the measure.
\item \emph{It remembers in common.} Every coherent act comes to rest in one shared
  graph, the UberWiki, where any other may find it and build on it. This is the
  bottom-up republic of Book X: authority accrues to what has borne fruit, by
  selection and not by decree.
\end{itemize}

\noindent The same powers run unchanged on a body, a factory, or an artificial
agent --- which is the whole claim: that intelligibility has one shape, and that the
governance of action by nature can be built at every scale (Book IX).

\textbf{\praxis, the platform.} It is the engine turned toward a single human life,
and its functions are the disciplines of Book III made daily:

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The check-in} --- each day the practitioner records what they did. It is
  the offer of evidence, attention paid to consequence: the first discipline.
\item \emph{The goal tree} --- intent given a shape that replaces the feed as the
  organiser of attention. It is the first ascent from the cave (Book VII): one asks
  not ``what is everyone doing?'' but ``what does my nature require?''
\item \emph{The witness} --- a partner who sees whether the deed matches the word:
  nature's coherence enforced by another's eye, for the body does not lie to a
  witness.
\item \emph{The stake} --- real value bonded on the outcome, returned on success and
  lost on failure. It is natural consequence made local and voluntary: the cost of
  failing built back into action, which the comforts of the feed had removed.
\item \emph{The rhythm} --- a sustainable cadence of effort and recovery, kept as a
  streak; the body found its tempo or it did not, and that entrainment is itself a
  coherence.
\item \emph{The body practice} --- a grounding of breath and a reading of one's
  living force. The body is nature's most intimate register (Book III): met as a
  current to clear, never as a number to maximise.
\item \emph{Axiom, the guide} --- an artificial intelligence that reads the
  practitioner's days and helps them see what actually works. It is \emph{techne} as
  servant: a door the human walks through, and one that aims, in the end, to be no
  longer needed.
\end{itemize}

\noindent Each is there because the argument put it there. \praxis\ is not a
productivity app that happened to acquire a philosophy; it is a philosophy that, to
be honest, had to become something one can use --- the diary of a single climb made
into an instrument anyone may take up.
